DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------
Grow, Track, and Nurture Your Garden Through Every Season

This gardening journal gives you the tools to plan, plant, and
maintain a thriving garden year after year. Whether you grow
vegetables, flowers, herbs, or houseplants, this journal helps
you track what works, learn from what doesn't, and become a
better gardener with each season.

Each two-page plant record lets you capture the plant name,
variety, type, source, date planted, location, sunlight and
water needs, soil conditions, growth milestones, care log,
pest and harvest records, and personal observations. With
30 plant entries, you'll have room for your entire garden.

Perfect for home gardeners, vegetable growers, flower
enthusiasts, herb gardeners, indoor plant collectors, and
homesteaders. An excellent gift for new homeowners, Mother's
Day, Father's Day, or anyone who loves watching things grow.

WHAT'S INSIDE

30 Two-Page Plant Record Spreads
Each spread captures full plant details (name, variety, type,
source, price, planting date, location), growing conditions
(sunlight, water, soil type, pH, spacing, depth), a care log
for watering and feeding, pest and problem tracking, harvest
records, and freeform notes.

Garden Layout Map Pages
Grid-based sketch pages for mapping your beds, rows, and plant
locations. Plan crop rotation and avoid overcrowding.

12 Monthly Task Planners
Each month includes weather notes (temperature, rainfall,
sunlight) and a 12-item task checklist with target dates.
Stay on top of seasonal chores from seed-starting to harvest.

Harvest Log (3 pages)
Track every harvest by crop, date, quantity, unit, and
quality notes. See your total yield at a glance.

Garden Expense Tracker
Monitor spending on seeds, soil, tools, fertilizers, pots,
and supplies by category.

Seed & Supply Order Tracker
Plan and track seed orders with variety, source, quantity,
price, and received status.

Pest & Disease Tracker
Log problems early with date, plant affected, issue
identified, treatment applied, and resolution status.

Reference Pages
USDA Hardiness Zone guide with temperature ranges and crop
recommendations. Seasonal planting calendar with tasks for
spring, summer, fall, and winter. Companion planting guide
showing good neighbors and plants to avoid.
