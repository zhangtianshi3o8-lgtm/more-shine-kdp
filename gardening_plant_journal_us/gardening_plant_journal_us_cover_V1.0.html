Grow, track, and nurture your garden through every season. This gardening journal gives you the tools to plan, plant, and maintain a thriving garden year after year. With 30 detailed plant record pages, monthly task planners, a harvest log, and a seasonal calendar, it is everything you need to become a better gardener.
What's Inside
  • 30 two-page plant record spreads with full details and care logs
  • 12 monthly task planners with weather tracking
  • Garden layout grid pages for mapping your beds
  • Harvest log to track yield by crop, date, and quantity
  • Seed and supply order tracker
  • Pest and disease log for early problem identification
  • Companion planting and USDA hardiness zone references
  • Large 6" x 9" format — easy to write in
