DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------
Every Loaf Has a Story.

This sourdough baking log is your companion for mastering artisan
bread at home. From starter management to the final bake, capture
every detail that makes each loaf unique — hydration, fermentation
time, temperature, scoring pattern, and crumb results.

Each bake uses a clean two-page spread: the left page records the
full formula and process — flour blend, total flour weight,
hydration percentage, water and salt amounts, levain build, bulk
fermentation time, cold proof hours, baking temperatures, vessel
type, and loaf shape. Six quality rating circles let you score
oven spring, crust, crumb, flavor, texture, and overall result.
The right page dives deeper with 12-item crumb, crust, and flavor
description checklists, scoring notes, what went well, what to
change next time, and an overall star rating.

Perfect for sourdough home bakers, artisan bread enthusiasts, and
anyone on a journey to bake better bread. An excellent gift for
the bread lover in your life.

WHAT'S INSIDE

40 Two-Page Bake Logs
Each session gives you space to record the date, loaf name, flour
blend, total flour weight, hydration, water, salt, levain amounts,
ambient and dough temperature, bulk fermentation time, cold proof
hours, baking temperatures (covered and uncovered), bake time,
shape, and vessel — plus 1-5 quality ratings for oven spring,
crust, crumb, flavor, texture, and overall.

Crumb, Crust & Flavor Checklists
12-item crumb description (open/holey, tight/even, gummy, moist,
tender, chewy, cottony, translucent, irregular, dense, custard-like,
dry), 10-item crust description, and 10-item flavor profile
checklists to precisely capture each loaf's character.

Baker's Percentage Guide
Understand the formula system used by professional bakers. Flour
is always 100%, and every other ingredient is a percentage of flour
weight. Includes a standard sourdough formula table and scaling
example.

Sourdough Process Reference
Seven-stage process explained: levain, autolyse, mix, bulk
fermentation, shape, cold proof, and bake. Signs of good
fermentation checklist.

Two-Day Baking Timeline
A complete hour-by-hour schedule from starter feeding through
cooling the finished loaf.

Flour Types Guide
Six common flour types with protein content and best use:
bread flour, all-purpose, whole wheat, rye, spelt, and einkorn.

Hydration Reference Table
Six hydration ranges from 60% to 85%+ with dough feel and
expected crumb results for each.

16-Term Sourdough Glossary
Autolyse, banneton, batard, boule, bulk ferment, cold proof,
crumb, ear, grigne, hydration, levain, oven spring, retard,
scoring, stretch & fold, and windowpane test.

Starter Feeding Log
Two pages to track starter health: date, time, temperature,
feeding ratio, rise time, and notes for 28 feedings.

Year-in-Review Favorites
Summarize your baking year: total loaves baked, best hydration
found, favorite flour blend, best crumb achievement, top-rated
loaf, and biggest baking lesson.
