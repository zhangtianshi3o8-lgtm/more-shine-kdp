DESCRIPTION (for Amazon listing)
==========================================================

Plan the wedding of your dreams—one beautiful detail at a time.

The Wedding Planner is your all-in-one companion for creating the perfect day without the stress. Whether you are planning an intimate ceremony or a grand celebration, this thoughtfully designed planner keeps every vendor, every dollar, and every decision beautifully organized.

Inside you will discover:

• 25 two-page vendor and planning spreads—capture company details, contact info, quotes, deposit status, and personal notes for every category from venue and caterer to florist, photographer, and beyond
• A stunning wedding overview page to record your date, venue, colors, theme, and what the day means to you as a couple
• A vision and inspiration section to define your dream atmosphere, must-have moments, and decor ideas
• A comprehensive budget tracker with 17 categories, deposit tracking, and payment scheduling
• 5 milestone review pages to reflect on progress, check stress levels, and set goals for each planning phase
• A guest list manager with space for 100+ guests, including RSVP tracking, plus-one status, and meal preferences
• A seating arrangement planner with table-by-table guest assignment
• A detailed day-of timeline covering every moment from hair and makeup to the grand send-off
• A wedding party roster for bridesmaids, groomsmen, ushers, and special roles
• A final countdown checklist for 2 weeks, 1 week, day-before, and morning-of tasks

Premium Design Features:
★ Elegant charcoal and gold Moleskine-inspired aesthetic
★ Generous 8" x 10" format with ample writing space
★ Lay-flat binding compatible design
★ High-quality cream paper for smooth writing
★ Section dividers, rating dots, and visual budget bars
★ Gold-accent interior with timeless Georgia serif typography

Perfect for engagements, bridal showers, or as a gift for the newly engaged couple.

Plan with love. Celebrate with joy. Cherish every moment.
