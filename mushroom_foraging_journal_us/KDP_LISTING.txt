DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------
The Perfect Companion for Every Mushroom Foraging Adventure

Whether you're a seasoned forager or just beginning to explore
the world of wild mushrooms, this beautifully designed journal
helps you document every specimen you encounter — from the
first morels of spring to the last chanterelles of fall. Every
find tells a story, and this journal gives you the structure to
capture it.

WHAT'S INSIDE

40 Two-Page Foraging Logs
Each entry gives you space to record date, weather, location,
GPS coordinates, habitat type, and tree associations — plus
full specimen identification fields including common and
scientific name, cap description, gill/pore type, spore print
color, stem characteristics, ring/volva presence, and odor.
The facing page includes edibility assessment, look-alike
notes, detailed observations, experience rating, and space
for next-time reminders.

Mushroom Anatomy & Identification Guide
Eight anatomical features explained: cap, gills/pores/teeth,
spore print color, stem, ring (annulus), volva (cup), flesh,
and odor — with practical tips for recording each accurately.

Habitat Guide
Eight common North American habitats profiled: deciduous
forest, coniferous forest, mixed forest, grasslands, wood
chips, burn sites, decaying wood, and wetlands — with key
species for each.

Foraging Safety Rules
Six essential safety rules plus the Golden Rule of foraging:
never eat a wild mushroom unless you can identify it with 100%
certainty. Includes Poison Control number (1-800-222-1222).

Seasonal Fruiting Calendar
Four-season guide to what fruits when — from spring morels to
winter oysters — with key species for each season.

Species Checklist
Three pages to build your life list, with 24 pre-listed common
North American species (morels, chanterelles, boletes, chicken
of the woods, and more) plus blank rows for your own finds.

Spot & Location Log
Three pages to track your favorite foraging spots — location,
habitat, best season, key species, and a "My Best Spot" section.

Foraging Kit & Equipment
One page for your gear inventory — baskets, cutting tools,
field guides, navigation, and essentials.

Year-in-Review Favorites
Summarize your foraging year: total species found, forays
made, spots explored, top 5 finds, personal milestones, and
what to hunt next season.

Sketch Pad & Notes
A dot-grid page for specimen sketches and spot maps, plus 10
pages of lined notes for species to research, recipes, and
reminders.
