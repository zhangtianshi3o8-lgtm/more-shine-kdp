DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------
The Perfect Companion for Every Hiking Adventure

Whether you're a weekend day-hiker or a dedicated peak-bagger,
this pocket-size journal helps you capture every trail experience.
From easy nature walks to challenging summit pushes, every hike
tells a story worth remembering.

WHAT'S INSIDE

40 Two-Page Trail Log Spreads
Each hike gives you space for trailhead location, weather
conditions, trail difficulty and surface type, distance, pace,
elevation gain, max elevation, calories burned, and heart rate —
plus trail highlights, wildlife spotted, and sketch space on the
facing page.

Leave No Trace Reference Card
The seven principles every hiker should know, printed for quick
reference before every trip.

The Ten Essentials
Classic hiking safety checklist — navigation, sun protection,
insulation, illumination, first aid, fire, repair kit, nutrition,
hydration, and emergency shelter.

Packing Checklist
11 categories with checkboxes for everything from boots to first
aid to food and water.

Year-in-Review Summit Summary
Track your total hikes, total miles, total elevation gained,
longest hike, highest elevation, and top 5 trails.
