DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------
Track Your Training and Crush Every Goal

This workout log gives you the space to record every rep,
every set, and every workout. Whether you lift weights, do
bodyweight training, run, or mix it all together, this journal
helps you stay accountable and keep progressing.

Each single-page workout log includes space for date, day of
week, workout focus (with checkboxes for push, pull, legs,
upper, lower, full body, cardio, and core), duration, and an
8-exercise tracking table with 5 sets per exercise. There is
also space for cardio and warm-up tracking, notes, personal
records, energy level, and an overall rating.

Perfect for gym-goers, weightlifters, runners, CrossFit
enthusiasts, home workout devotees, personal training clients,
and anyone serious about fitness. An excellent gift for New
Year's resolutions, fitness beginners, athletes, and anyone
ready to transform.

WHAT'S INSIDE

100 Single-Page Workout Logs
Each page tracks date, day, focus, and duration, with an
8-exercise table (5 sets each), cardio section, notes, energy
level, and star rating. Focus checkboxes cover all major
training splits.

Body Measurement Tracker
12-month tracking for weight, chest, waist, hips, arm, thigh,
and body fat percentage. Starting and goal weight cards.

Goal Setting Page
Pick your primary goals, set specific targets with current and
target values plus deadlines, and plan your weekly schedule.

Personal Records Tracker
Track 1RM, 3RM, and 5RM for 12 key lifts from bench press to
plank. Space for PR highlights and milestones.

Weekly Review Pages (4)
Reflect on what worked, what to improve, and set goals for
the coming week. Track daily training status and weekly stats.
