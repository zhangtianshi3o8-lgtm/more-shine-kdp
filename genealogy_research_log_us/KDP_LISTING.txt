DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------
Every Family Has a Story Worth Preserving.

This genealogy research log book helps you organize your family
history research systematically. Whether you are just starting
your family tree or have years of experience, this journal keeps
every ancestor, every source, and every discovery in one place.

Each ancestor gets a dedicated two-page spread: the left page
captures vital statistics (birth, death, marriage, parents,
children, occupation, military service). The right page records
your research sources, key findings, next steps, and family
stories.

With census tracker pages and a family tree index, you can see
migration patterns across decades and keep track of every branch
of your family.

Perfect for genealogy beginners, experienced family historians,
and anyone passionate about preserving their heritage.

No software, no subscriptions. Just a durable, thoughtfully
designed log book that becomes a permanent family record.

WHAT'S INSIDE

25 Two-Page Ancestor Profile Spreads
Each profile gives you space for full name, aliases, birth/death
dates and places, marriage details, parents, children, occupation,
religion, and military service -- plus a source log, findings,
and next steps.

Family Tree Index
A 14-row index to list every ancestor you research with their key
dates and page references.

Census Tracker Pages
Organize census findings by family across multiple decades --
location, age, occupation, film numbers, and migration notes.

Surname and Region Tracking
Pages to track the family names and geographic regions you are
researching.
