Craft, record, and perfect every drink you make. This cocktail journal gives you the space to develop your mixology skills and build a personal repertoire of signature drinks. With dedicated recipe pages, a flavor wheel, classic recipes, and a home bar inventory, it is everything you need to elevate your home bartending.
What's Inside
  • 40 two-page recipe spreads with ingredients and ratings
  • Flavor balance scale (sweet, sour, boozy, bitter)
  • 12 classic cocktail recipes with measurements
  • Base spirits, techniques, and glassware reference
  • Home bar inventory with stock-level tracking
  • Equipment and glassware collection checklists
  • Large 6" x 9" format — easy to write in
