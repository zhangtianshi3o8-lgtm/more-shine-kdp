DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------
Craft, Record, and Perfect Every Drink You Make

This cocktail journal gives you the space to develop your
mixology skills and build a personal repertoire of signature
drinks. Whether you're a weekend host, an aspiring home
bartender, or a cocktail enthusiast exploring the craft, this
journal helps you track what works and refine your technique.

Each two-page recipe spread lets you record the cocktail name,
base spirit, glass, method, ingredients with measurements,
step-by-step instructions, flavor balance ratings, an overall
star rating, and your tasting notes. Classic recipes, spirits
guide, techniques reference, and a home bar inventory make
this a complete bartending companion.

Perfect for home bartenders, cocktail enthusiasts, aspiring
mixologists, dinner party hosts, and anyone who loves creating
great drinks. An excellent gift for newlyweds, new homeowners,
or anyone building a home bar.

WHAT'S INSIDE

40 Two-Page Recipe Spreads
Each spread gives you space for the cocktail name, base spirit,
glass type, method (shake/stir/build/blender), full ingredients
list with measurements, step-by-step directions, a flavor
balance scale (sweet, sour, boozy, bitter, complexity, finish),
star rating, and tasting notes.

Cocktail Flavor Wheel
Eight categories with 60+ tasting notes — from citrus and sour
to herbal, bitter, smoky, and tropical. Find the exact words for
the flavors you experience.

Base Spirits Guide
Eight major spirits explained: Vodka, Gin, Rum, Tequila,
Whiskey, Brandy & Cognac, Mezcal, and Liqueurs & Aperitifs —
with characteristics and best-for guidance.

Essential Techniques
Eight fundamental techniques defined: shake, stir, muddle,
build, blend, layer, double strain, and fat wash.

Glassware Guide
Eight glass types profiled: coupe, martini, rocks, highball,
Nick & Nora, hurricane, margarita, and snifter.

Classic Cocktail Recipes
Tw timeless recipes with exact measurements: Old Fashioned,
Negroni, Margarita, Martini, Manhattan, Daiquiri, Mojito,
Whiskey Sour, Mai Tai, Cosmopolitan, Aperol Spritz, and
Moscow Mule.

Home Bar Inventory
Three inventory pages to track every bottle by category,
volume, stock level, and notes — so you always know what to
restock.

Equipment & Glassware Collection
Log your shakers, jiggers, strainers, and glassware. Check
off 20 essential glass types as you build your collection.
