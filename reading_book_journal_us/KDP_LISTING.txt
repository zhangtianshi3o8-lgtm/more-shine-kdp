DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------
Track Every Book You Read and Every Story You Love

This reading journal gives you the space to capture your
thoughts, quotes, and impressions for every book you
experience. Whether you read fiction, nonfiction, romance,
fantasy, or mysteries, this journal helps you build a lasting
personal library of thought.

Each two-page review spread lets you record the title, author,
genre, page count, start and finish dates, format, and source.
Rate each aspect — plot, characters, writing style, pacing,
emotional impact — on a five-star scale. Write your spoiler-free
summary, copy your favorite quotes with page numbers, note
memorable characters and themes, and capture your full review.

Perfect for avid readers, book club members, lifelong learners,
students, and anyone who loves stories. An excellent gift for
readers, graduates, bookworms, teachers, and the holidays.

WHAT'S INSIDE

50 Two-Page Book Review Spreads
Each spread includes full book details (title, author, genre,
pages, publisher, year, dates, format, source), an overall
star rating, six sub-ratings (plot, characters, writing,
pacing, emotional impact, recommend), spoiler-free summary,
favorite quotes with page numbers, memorable characters,
themes and takeaways, and a full review space.

Reading Goal Tracker
Set your annual reading goal and mark each book on a 50-book
progress grid. Track monthly progress with a 12-month table.

Quick Book Log (3 pages)
A one-line-per-book summary table for a bird's-eye view of
everything you've read — title, genre, date, and rating.

Reading Wishlist (2 pages)
Track books you want to read with title, genre, priority
level, and a checkbox for when you've completed them.

Book Club Tracker
Record book club meetings with book title, date, host, and
discussion highlights.

Year-in-Review
Celebrate your reading year with stats (books read, pages
read, new authors), top 5 books, and personal discoveries
across five categories.

Genre Guide
Ten genres explained with descriptions and notable authors —
from literary fiction to poetry and essays.
