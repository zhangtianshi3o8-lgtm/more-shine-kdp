DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------
Every Hunt Tells a Story.

From the first light of opening day to the last sit of the season,
this hunting log book helps you capture every detail that matters.
Weather conditions, wind direction, game sightings, shot placement,
harvest details, and the field notes that turn a good hunt into a
great story.

Each hunt uses a clean two-page spread designed for the field. The
left page records date, location, game species, weather, barometric
pressure, moon phase, and game sighting details. The right page
captures shot records, harvest details, weapon and gear used, and
your personal field notes and lessons learned.

With season summary tables every seven hunts, you can track your
season performance by species and spot patterns over time. Perfect
for deer, elk, bear, turkey, waterfowl, upland bird, predator, and
small game hunters.

Built tough enough to throw in your pack, detailed enough to become
a lifetime record of your hunting seasons.

WHAT'S INSIDE

35 Two-Page Hunt Log Spreads
Each hunt captures date, location, state/zone, hunt type, time in
and out, game species checkboxes (8 categories), detailed weather
(temp, wind, conditions, barometric pressure, moon phase, visibility),
game sighting details with behavior and distance, and animal activity
rating.

Shot Record & Harvest Details
The right page of each log includes a two-shot record with distance
and shot placement, harvest confirmation, species and sex, estimated
weight, points/antlers, weapon and gear log, field notes, and lessons
learned for next time.

Season Summary Tables
Every seven hunts, a summary table breaks down your season by species
— days hunted, sightings, harvested, and notes — so you can spot
patterns and plan better next year.

Season Goals & License Tracking
Dedicated front pages for planning your season: species targets,
locations, license and tag numbers, gear wish list, and personal goals.
