DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------
Every Pint Has a Story.

This beer tasting journal is your companion for exploring the world
of craft beer. From the first pour to the last sip, capture the
character of every beer you taste — from crisp pilsners to bold
imperial stouts, tart sours to hop-forward IPAs.

Each tasting uses a clean two-page spread: the left page records beer
details (name, brewery, style, ABV, IBU, color, serving type) and
your 1-5 sensory ratings across six categories. The right page dives
deeper with a 20-note flavor checklist, appearance notes, aroma
impressions, freeform tasting notes, overall star rating, and food
pairing suggestions.

Perfect for craft beer enthusiasts, homebrewers, taproom regulars,
and anyone who wants to document their beer journey. An excellent
gift for beer lovers and aspiring cicerones.

WHAT'S INSIDE

40 Two-Page Tasting Spreads
Each session gives you space to record the beer name, brewery, style,
ABV, IBU, SRM/color, serving type (draft, bottle, or can), date,
location, and price — plus 1-5 rating circles for hop bitterness,
malt sweetness, body, carbonation, aroma, and finish. The facing page
includes a 20-note flavor checklist, appearance notes, aroma notes,
freeform tasting notes, star rating, "would drink again" checkboxes,
and a food pairing suggestion.

Beer Flavor Wheel
Eight categories with 48+ tasting notes — Hoppy, Malty, Roasted,
Fruity, Spice, Sour/Funky, Sweet, and Other. Find the exact words
for what you taste.

Beer Style Guide
Twelve major styles explained with characteristics and ABV/IBU
ranges: IPA, Pale Ale, Stout, Porter, Pilsner, Wheat, Saison,
Belgian, Sour, Lager, Brown Ale, and Barleywine.

SRM Color Reference
Understand the Standard Reference Method color scale from pale straw
to black, so you can accurately describe what you see.

Glassware Guide
Six common beer glasses explained — Pint, Tulip, Snifter, Weizen,
Pilsner, and Goblet — with what each is best for.

Brewery Tracker
Two pages to log your favorite breweries with location, favorite beer,
and personal rating.

Tasting Terminology
Twelve essential terms defined: ABV, IBU, SRM, Gravity, Attenuation,
Head, Lace, Body, Conditioning, Dry-Hopping, Session Beer, and
Imperial.

Year-in-Review Favorites
Summarize your beer year: total tastings, styles explored, top beer,
favorite brewery, biggest surprise, and most memorable pour.
