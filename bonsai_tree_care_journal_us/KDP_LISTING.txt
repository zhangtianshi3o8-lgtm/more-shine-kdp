Nurture Every Tree, Track Every Season

The Bonsai Tree Care Journal is a dedicated companion for
the slow art of bonsai. Whether you tend a single juniper
or a collection of forty trees, this journal helps you
document each tree's identity, track every watering and
pruning session, monitor health through the seasons, and
watch your skills grow alongside your collection.

WHAT'S INSIDE:

40 Tree Records — Each tree gets a full two-page spread:
  Left page: Date, acquisition record, species, common
  name, source, physical description (style, age, height,
  trunk caliper), pot details, design vision, and a photo/
  sketch area for documenting current appearance.
  Right page: Work performed checklist (watering,
  fertilizing, pruning, pinching, wiring, wire removal,
  repotting, root pruning, defoliation, repositioning,
  pest treatment, winterizing), care details (fertilizer,
  soil mix, wire gauge, weather), health ratings for
  foliage/roots/bark on 1-5 scale, overall star rating,
  pest/concern notes, and observation/next-step notes.

Reference Section (8 pages):
  • 15 common bonsai species quick-reference chart
  • Seasonal pruning calendar (spring/summer/fall/winter)
  • Watering fundamentals and troubleshooting guide
  • Soil mixtures and repotting guide with ratios
  • Wiring techniques, application steps, removal timing
  • Pest and disease identification chart (8 common issues)
  • 10 classical bonsai styles reference

Collection Management:
  • Collection overview inventory (48 entries, 4 pages)
  • Seasonal task calendar with checkboxes
  • Tools, wire stock, and soil supplies inventory (2 pages)
  • Exhibition and show records
  • Year-in-review favorites and personal milestones
  • 2 dot-grid sketch pages for design planning
  • 5 pages of lined notes

DETAILS:
  • 110 pages, large 6" x 9" format
  • Professional interior design with moss green and
    terracotta accents
  • Easy-to-write-in layout with generous spacing
  • Cream paper, perfect binding
  • Suitable for beginners and experienced bonsai artists

Patience is the greatest tool. This journal helps you
use it wisely.
