DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------
Some loves leave paw prints on our hearts forever.

This memorial journal offers a gentle, private space to honor
the life of a beloved pet — whether furry, feathered, or scaled.
Remember their personality, their quirks, and the joy they
brought to every day. A universal keepsake for any companion
animal who touched your life.

WHAT'S INSIDE

My Pet's Story
Guided profile pages to record your pet's name, species,
breed or type, appearance, personality traits, nicknames,
and the story of how you met. Rate 12 personality traits
and capture their funniest, naughtiest, and smartest moments.

8 Memory Spreads
Each two-page spread gives you space for a favorite photo,
a caption, the full story of what happened, and how it made
you feel. Eight treasured memories to revisit whenever you
need to feel close to them again.

Photo Gallery
Six gallery pages with room for 36+ photos and captions —
every side of their personality, preserved forever.

Letters & Reflections
Write letters directly to your pet. Reflect on what they
taught you about love, patience, and joy. Find comfort in
words from Anatole France, George Eliot, and Charles Darwin.

The Final Goodbye
Honor the farewell with a dedicated section for the details
of that day, a promise to your pet, resting place and memorial
information, and a reflection on their legacy.

Paw Print Keepsake
A special page with a decorative frame to preserve an ink
paw print — a lasting impression of their little paw.

Notes & Sketch Pad
A dot-grid sketch page plus 16 lined pages for poems,
quotes, drawings, and any other thoughts you wish to record.
