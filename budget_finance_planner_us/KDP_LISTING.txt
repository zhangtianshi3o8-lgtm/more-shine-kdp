DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------
Take Control of Your Money, One Month at a Time

This budget and finance planner gives you the tools to plan,
track, and achieve your financial goals. Whether you are
starting your budgeting journey, working to pay off debt,
or saving for a major milestone, this planner keeps
everything organized in one place.

Each two-page monthly spread lets you record income from
multiple sources, plan expenses by category, track actual
spending, and review your progress. Weekly expense trackers
help you log every purchase, while savings goals, debt
payoff, and net worth pages give you the full picture of
your financial health.

Perfect for individuals, couples, and families who want to
take charge of their finances, college students learning to
manage money, and anyone working toward financial freedom.
An excellent gift for graduates, newlyweds, new homeowners,
or anyone ready to transform their relationship with money.

WHAT'S INSIDE

12 Monthly Two-Page Budget Spreads
Each spread includes an income section (primary salary,
secondary, freelance, other), a planned expense breakdown
by 12 categories, and an actual expense tracker with
variance calculations. A monthly summary shows income,
expenses, and remaining balance at a glance.

6 Weekly Expense Trackers
Log every purchase by day, description, category, and
amount. Daily granularity reveals spending patterns and
helps you stay accountable to your monthly plan.

Savings Goals Tracker
Track 8 savings goals with target amounts, amounts saved,
target dates, and visual progress bars. From emergency
funds to vacation savings, watch your progress grow.

Debt Payoff Tracker
Use the snowball method to list 8 debts from smallest to
largest. Record creditor, balance, interest rate, and
minimum payment, then track your payoff progress.

Bills & Subscription Tracker
Track 18 recurring bills with due dates, amounts,
frequency, auto-pay status, and payment confirmation.

Net Worth Statement
A complete financial snapshot — assets minus liabilities —
to track your overall financial health over time.

Annual Overview & Year-End Review
See your full year at a glance, then reflect on wins,
lessons, and goals for the year ahead.

Reference Pages
Budgeting methods guide (50/30/20, zero-based, envelope
system, and more), expense category reference, and SMART
goal-setting framework.
