DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------
The Ultimate Companion for Every Beekeeping Journey

Whether you're a new beekeeper starting your first package or an
experienced apiarist managing multiple yards, this beautifully
designed log book helps you track every hive inspection with
precision and care. From spring buildup to fall harvest, your
colonies' health and productivity deserve to be documented.

WHAT'S INSIDE

40 Two-Page Hive Inspection Spreads
Each inspection gives you space to record date, weather, colony
status (queen, eggs, larvae, capped brood, swarm cells), temperament,
brood pattern, honey and pollen stores, varroa mite counts, and all
management actions taken — plus notes and next steps on the facing page.

Hive Anatomy & Components Guide
8 essential hive parts explained — from outer cover to bottom board.

Seasonal Beekeeping Calendar
Spring through winter: what to monitor, what to do, and what to
watch for in each season.

Nectar & Pollen Plant Checklist
24 common honey plants with bloom tracking, plus space for your
local forage discoveries.

Equipment Checklist
Complete gear reference organized by category: hive components,
tools, protective wear, feeding, health, and harvest equipment.

Honey Harvest & Year-in-Review Summary
Track total honey weight, colony count, inspection count, and
swarm events — with harvest log table and reflection space.
