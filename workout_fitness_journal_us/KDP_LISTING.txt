DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------
Every Rep Counts. Every Set Matters.

This workout journal is your training partner. Whether you lift weights,
do bodyweight training, or mix cardio and strength, this journal gives
you the structure to log 40 complete training sessions with full
set-by-set tracking.

Each workout uses a clean two-page spread: the left page captures your
exercise table with sets, reps, weights, and RPE (how hard each set
felt). The right page tracks cardio, energy and strength levels,
personal records, and your focus for next time.

Track your body measurements, one-rep max progress, and personal records
over time. With weekly progress summaries every four workouts, you will
spot trends and stay motivated as the numbers climb.

Built for serious lifters, beginners, and everyone in between. No apps,
no batteries, no subscriptions. Just you, your pen, and your training.

Perfect for weightlifters, bodybuilders, powerlifters, CrossFit athletes,
and anyone committed to getting stronger.

WHAT'S INSIDE

40 Two-Page Workout Log Spreads
Each session gives you space for the date, workout type (Push, Pull,
Legs, Upper, Lower, Full Body, Cardio, or Sports), an exercise table
with six rows for sets/reps/weight/RPE, and warm-up notes.

Cardio & Recovery Tracking
The right page of each log includes cardio type, duration, distance,
heart rate, 1-10 ratings for energy, strength, and motivation, personal
records for the day, and your focus for the next session.

Body Measurement Tracker
Record 11 body measurements at four checkpoints — start, month 1,
month 2, and month 3. Includes progress photo date log.

One-Rep Max Progress
Track your 1RM for bench press, squat, deadlift, overhead press, barbell
row, and power clean with start, current, goal, and date fields.

Weekly Progress Summaries
Every four workouts, a summary page helps you review changes in body
weight, body fat percentage, waist, and total workouts completed.

Personal Records History
Two full pages to log every PR with date, lift, weight, reps, and notes.

Exercise Reference
Common exercises organized by muscle group — Chest, Back, Shoulders,
Legs, Arms, and Core — for workout-planning inspiration.

Fitness Glossary
Twelve essential terms defined: RPE, 1RM, AMRAP, DOMS, hypertrophy,
compound lift, isolation lift, superset, drop set, progressive overload,
deload, and volume.
