DESCRIPTION
==========================================================

Never forget a password again.

The Password Keeper is an elegant offline vault for organizing every online account, login, and PIN in one secure place. With alphabetical A-Z tabbed pages and spacious entry tables, finding and updating your credentials has never been easier -- no apps, no subscriptions, no digital hacks to worry about.

Inside you will discover:

* A-Z alphabetical tabs with 300+ entry spaces for websites and apps
* Each entry records: website name, username, password, email used, security question and answer, and notes
* PIN and code reference section for devices, alarms, and services
* Subscriptions and recurring bills tracker with renewal dates
* Personal security info page -- Wi-Fi, alarm codes, recovery emails
* Security audit checklist with best practices for password hygiene
* Notes pages for backup codes and recovery information

Premium Design:
★ Elegant charcoal and gold padlock motif
★ A-Z tab organization for instant lookup
★ Generous 8" x 10" format with wide entry rows
★ High-quality cream paper for smooth writing

Perfect for anyone who wants to stay organized and secure -- from tech-savvy users to parents and grandparents who prefer a physical logbook over digital apps.
