DESCRIPTION (for Amazon listing)
------------------------------------------------------------
Every ride tells a story. This journal helps you remember each one.

Whether you ride road, mountain, gravel, or commuter routes, the Cycling Adventure Journal gives you a clean, organized way to record every detail that matters — distance, elevation, weather, route notes, and how the ride felt.

<b>What's Inside:</b>

  - 35 two-page ride spreads with comprehensive prompts
  - Route details: distance, time, elevation gain, average speed
  - Weather and surface conditions tracking
  - Bike setup and gear notes for each ride
  - Personal highlights and memorable moments section
  - Milestone tracker for personal records and goals
  - Annual summary and statistics pages
  - Compact 5" x 8" size — fits in a jersey pocket or saddlebag

<b>Designed for Cyclists:</b>

The two-page-per-ride layout gives you room to breathe. Left page captures the hard data — distance, time, climbing, conditions. Right page is for the story — what you saw, how you felt, what you'd do differently. No clutter, just clean space that respects your riding habits.

<b>Perfect For:</b>
  - Road cyclists tracking training progress
  - Mountain bikers logging trail adventures
  - Bikepackers and touring cyclists
  - Commuters building a riding habit
  - Gifts for the cyclist in your life
