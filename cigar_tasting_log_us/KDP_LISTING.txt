DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------
The Ultimate Companion for Every Cigar Tasting Journey

Whether you're a weekend aficionado or a seasoned connoisseur,
this beautifully designed log book helps you capture every nuance
of every smoke. From creamy Connecticut wrappers to bold Maduro
blends, your cigar journey deserves to be documented with care.

WHAT'S INSIDE

40 Two-Page Tasting Spreads
Each session gives you space to record the cigar brand, origin
country, wrapper leaf, vitola shape, ring gauge, length, age,
and strength — plus a 20-note flavor checklist with ratings for
smoke volume, sweetness, earth/wood, spice, body, and finish length.

Cigar Flavor Wheel
8 categories: earth and wood, sweet, spice, nutty, coffee and
cocoa, fruit, floral and herbal, leather and smoke.

Cigar Types (Vitolas) Guide
8 common shapes from Corona to Gordo, each with dimensions
and best-for guidance.

12 Cigar Regions Reference
Cuba, Nicaragua, Dominican Republic, Honduras, and more — each
with characteristic flavor profile.

Tasting Terminology Glossary
8 essential terms: body, draw, burn, ash, finish, retrohale,
strength, and complexity.
