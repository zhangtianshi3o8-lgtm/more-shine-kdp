Description:

Track Every Batch, Every Culture, Every Flavor

The Fermentation Journal is your personal companion for the
ancient art of living food. Whether you are making tangy
sauerkraut, fizzy kombucha, fiery hot sauce, or rich miso,
this journal turns experiments into reproducible recipes
and transforms trial and error into genuine expertise.

WHAT'S INSIDE:

40 Batch Logs — Each batch gets a full two-page spread:
  Left page: date, batch name, ferment type, culture/starter
  source, ingredients with weights (grams), salt percentage,
  brine ratio, and vessel size.
  Right page: daily fermentation timeline (day 1-30+ with
  temp, pH, and observations), sensory ratings (sourness,
  saltiness, crunch/texture, complexity), overall star
  rating, outcome checkboxes, tasting notes, and
  adjustment ideas.

Reference Section:
  • Salt and brine guide with precise ratios for every type
  • pH and timeline reference (target pH, duration, temp)
  • Types of fermentation (6 categories, 40+ ferments listed)
  • Safety guidelines (mold vs kahm yeast, botulism, hygiene)

Tracking Pages:
  • Starter culture registry (36 entries: SCOBYs, grains,
    sourdough starters, koji)
  • Recipe development worksheets (2 pages for your own
    signature ferments)
  • Equipment and gear inventory
  • Supplier and ingredient source directory (24 entries)
  • Tasting journal for finished ferments (16 entries)
  • Year-in-review favorites and personal discoveries
  • Dot grid sketch pad for recipe mapping
  • 6 pages of lined notes

DETAILS:
  • 110 pages, large 6" x 9" format
  • Professional interior design with copper/amber accents
  • Easy-to-write-in layout with generous spacing
  • Cream paper, perfect binding
  • Suitable for beginners and experienced fermenters alike

Fermentation is both art and science. This journal helps
you master both, one batch at a time.
