DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------
The Ultimate Companion for Every Wine Tasting Journey

Whether you're a seasoned wine enthusiast or just beginning to
explore the world of wine, this beautifully designed journal
helps you capture every nuance of every glass. From crisp
Sauvignon Blanc to bold Cabernet Sauvignon, your tasting
journey deserves to be documented with care.

WHAT'S INSIDE

40 Two-Page Tasting Spreads
Each session gives you space to record the wine name, producer,
vintage, grape variety, region, ABV, and price — plus 1-5
rating circles for tannin, acidity, body, sweetness, alcohol,
and finish length. The facing page includes a 20-note flavor
checklist, nosing notes, star rating, food pairing suggestions,
and freeform tasting notes.

Wine Flavor Wheel
Eight categories with 50+ tasting notes — from red fruit and
dark fruit to oak, earth, floral, spice, citrus, and tropical.
Find the exact words for what you taste.

Wine Types Guide
Eight major styles explained: Red, White, Rosé, Sparkling,
Dessert, Fortified, Orange, and Natural — with characteristics
and best-for guidance.

Wine Regions Guide
Twelve regions profiled in detail: Bordeaux, Burgundy,
Champagne, Rhône, Tuscany, Piedmont, Rioja, Mosel, California,
Oregon, Barossa Valley, and Marlborough.

Tasting Terminology
Eight essential terms defined: nose, palate, finish, tannins,
acidity, body, terroir, and vintage.

Wine Cellar Tracker
Three pages to catalog your wine collection — wine, producer,
type, vintage, varietal, region, and personal rating.

Winery & Wine Shop Log
Two pages for recording your favorite wineries, wine shops,
and tasting rooms. Includes a "My Go-To Shop" section.

Glassware & Accessories
One page for your tasting setup — glassware, decanter, wine
tools, and accessories.

Wine Regions Checklist
Tick off regions as you taste your way around the wine world —
from Bordeaux to Napa Valley to Marlborough.

Year-in-Review Favorites
Summarize your wine year: total tastings, regions explored,
top 5 wines, personal discoveries, and what to explore next.

Sketch Pad & Notes
A dot-grid page for sketches and doodles, plus 10 pages of
lined notes for ideas, pairings, and reminders.
