Description:
========================================================
Track Every Blend, Every Oil, Every Benefit

The Essential Oil & Aromatherapy Journal is your personal
apothecary companion — designed for wellness seekers, essential
oil enthusiasts, and anyone who wants to get more out of their
aromatherapy practice.

WHAT'S INSIDE:

40 Blend Recipe Logs — Each blend gets a full two-page spread:
  Left page: date, name, purpose, application method, carrier
  oil, essential oils with drop counts, total drops, and
  dilution percentage.
  Right page: aroma profile ratings (top/middle/base notes,
  sweetness, strength, longevity), aroma character checklist,
  scent description, overall rating, and room for detailed
  observations and adjustments.

Reference Section:
  • Dilution guide with safe ratios for every use case
  • Essential oil categories (6 families, 40+ oils listed)
  • The art of blending — the note system and classic ratios
  • Safety guidelines for responsible oil use

Tracking Pages:
  • Oil collection inventory (36 slots across 3 pages)
  • Weekly wellness tracker (4 weeks of daily logging)
  • Single oil profiles (4 deep-dive templates)
  • Supplier and shop directory (24 entries)
  • Tools and accessories inventory
  • Year-in-review favorites summary
  • Dot grid sketch pad for blend mapping
  • 7 pages of lined notes

DETAILS:
  • 116 pages, large 6" x 9" format
  • Professional interior design with sage green accents
  • Easy-to-write-in layout with generous spacing
  • Cream paper, perfect binding
  • Suitable for beginners and experienced oil users alike

Whether you're just starting your essential oil journey or
have a collection of dozens of oils, this journal helps you
turn trial and error into genuine expertise — one blend at
a time.
