Catalog Every Specimen, Every Property, Every Meaning

The Gemstone & Crystal Collection Journal is your personal
crystal encyclopedia — a beautifully designed record book
that transforms a random collection into a curated reference.
Whether you are a casual collector, a crystal healing
practitioner, or a mineral enthusiast, this journal helps
you document every specimen's physical properties,
metaphysical associations, and your own personal
experiences.

WHAT'S INSIDE:

40 Specimen Logs — Each specimen gets a full two-page
spread:
  Left page: Date, catalog number, stone name, variety,
  acquisition record (source, location, price), physical
  properties (color, crystal system, Mohs hardness, luster,
  transparency, mineral class), weight, size, formation,
  and visual description.
  Right page: Chakra and element associations, zodiac and
  planetary correspondences, metaphysical keyword checklist
  (protection, grounding, love, calm, clarity, abundance,
  healing, intuition, creativity, communication, spiritual
  growth), preferred cleansing method, energy ratings
  (vibration and resonance on 1-5 scale), overall star
  rating, usage flags (favorite, daily carry, meditation,
  display, grid work), and personal experience notes.

Reference Section:
  • Mohs hardness scale (1-10) with care implications
  • Seven crystal systems with common examples
  • Chakra and crystal color association chart
  • Cleansing and charging methods guide (8 methods)
  • Safety and care guidelines (water-soluble, sunlight-
    fading, toxic minerals, fragile stones, storage)

Collection Management:
  • Collection overview inventory (48 entries across 4 pages)
  • Sources and dealers directory (24 entries)
  • Display and storage system planner
  • Crystal grid records (2 templates with layout sketch pad)
  • Meditation and crystal work session log (16 entries)
  • Year-in-review favorites and personal discoveries
  • Dot grid sketch pad for drawings and grid layouts
  • 6 pages of lined notes

DETAILS:
  • 112 pages, large 6" x 9" format
  • Professional interior design with amethyst purple and
    silver accents
  • Easy-to-write-in layout with generous spacing
  • Cream paper, perfect binding
  • Suitable for beginners and experienced collectors

Your collection tells a story. This journal helps you
remember every chapter.
