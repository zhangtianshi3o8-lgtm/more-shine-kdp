DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------
Preserve the Recipes That Bring Your Family Together

From Grandma's secret sauce to your own weeknight favorites,
every cherished dish deserves to be written down and passed on.
This beautifully designed recipe journal gives you the perfect
place to capture it all — in one heirloom-quality book you'll
treasure for years.

Whether you're a seasoned home cook, a new bride building your
kitchen library, or someone who wants to preserve family recipes
before they're lost, this journal makes it easy to record every
detail with care.

WHAT'S INSIDE

50 Two-Page Recipe Spreads
Each recipe gets a full spread. The left page gives you space for
the recipe name, who shared it with you, category, servings, prep
and cook times, difficulty rating, star rating, and 16 ingredient
lines with check boxes for mise en place. The right page provides
15 numbered step lines for directions, plus a notes section for
tips and variations, and a "date made" field.

Recipe Index (A-Z)
Five index pages organized alphabetically so you can find any
recipe in seconds. List each recipe by name and page number as
you add it.

Measurement Equivalents Guide
A handy reference page covering US dry and liquid measures, oven
temperature conversions (F / C / Gas Mark), and common pantry
weights — so you never have to look it up online.

Substitutions & Storage Guide
Emergency ingredient substitutions, cooking abbreviations, and
food storage guidelines for refrigerator and freezer.

Generous Writing Space
Three blank notes pages at the end for extra recipes, ideas, or
kitchen memories.
