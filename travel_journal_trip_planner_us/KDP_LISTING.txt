DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------
Plan Your Adventures and Capture Every Journey

This travel journal gives you the space to plan trips before
you go and record every moment while you're there. Whether
you're backpacking through Europe, relaxing on a tropical
beach, or exploring a new city, this journal helps you turn
fleeting experiences into lasting memories.

Each two-page trip log includes space for destination details,
travel dates, trip type, flight and accommodation info, a
budget tracker with planned vs. actual spending, a 5-day
daily log with weather, best meals, memorable moments, people
met, and an overall rating.

Perfect for backpackers, vacation planners, road trippers,
cruise travelers, families, solo travelers, and adventure
seekers. An excellent gift for graduates, honeymooners,
retirees, gap-year students, and anyone with wanderlust.

WHAT'S INSIDE

20 Two-Page Trip Log Spreads
Each spread captures full trip details (destination, country,
dates, trip type, travelers, flight info, accommodation) and
a budget tracker with six categories. The right page includes
a 5-day daily log, best meals, most memorable moment, people
met, and an overall star rating.

Travel Bucket List
25-entry wishlist with priority rankings and checkboxes.

Pre-Trip Planning Checklist
Three categories: documents and money, before-you-go tasks,
and home preparation.

Packing List
Four categories: essentials, clothing, toiletries, and extras
with checkboxes for each item.

Countries Visited Tracker
Track every country with year visited, cities explored, and
trip highlight. Includes stat summary cards.

World Continents Reference
Seven continents with notable destinations and landmarks.
