DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------
The Perfect Companion for Every Night Under the Stars

Whether you are a backyard beginner or a dedicated deep-sky
observer, this pocket-size journal helps you capture every
stargazing session. From quick constellation hunts to marathon
nights under dark skies, every observation is worth remembering.

WHAT'S INSIDE

35 Two-Page Observation Spreads
Each session gives you space for date, location, coordinates,
sky conditions, moon phase, seeing and transparency ratings,
Bortle scale, and start/end times — plus an objects observed
tracker table, eyepiece sketch circle, highlights, challenges,
and discovery notes on the facing page.

Constellation Guide
Twelve major constellations with brief descriptions to help
you find your way around the night sky, from Ursa Major to
Scorpius to Lyra.

Stargazing Essentials
Ten items every observer should bring, from a red light
flashlight to patience. Protect your night vision and stay
comfortable through long sessions.

Observation Targets Checklist
A lifetime checklist of must-see objects — solar system
targets from the Moon to Neptune, plus deep-sky highlights
including the Orion Nebula, Pleiades, and Andromeda Galaxy.

Year-in-Review Stargazing Summary
Track your total sessions, hours under the stars, objects
observed, new discoveries, and top 5 nights of the season.
