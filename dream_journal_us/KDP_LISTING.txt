DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------
Every Dream Is a Message From Within.

Whether you are exploring your subconscious, practicing lucid
dreaming, or simply curious about the worlds that unfold each
night, this journal helps you capture, interpret, and learn
from your dreams.

Each dream uses a clean two-page spread. The left page captures
the essentials: date, dream title, mood, lucidity level, dream
type, key symbols, and emotions felt. The right page gives you
ample space to write the full narrative -- every detail you
remember, in any order, without worrying about grammar or logic.

With a built-in dream symbols reference guide, lucid dreaming
progress tracker, and monthly summaries to spot recurring
patterns, this journal is the perfect companion for anyone
serious about understanding their inner world.

Perfect for dreamers, lucid dreamers, therapists, artists
seeking inspiration, and anyone fascinated by the mysteries
of the sleeping mind.

WHAT'S INSIDE

25 Two-Page Dream Record Spreads
Each dream records date, title, mood, lucidity level (0-5),
clarity rating (1-5), emotion intensity (1-5), dream type
(Vivid, Lucid, Recurring, Nightmare, Prophetic, Fragment,
Epic, Healing), key symbols, and emotions. The facing page
provides generous lined space for the full dream narrative,
plus sections for interpretation and waking-life connections.

Dream Symbols Reference Guide
A curated reference of 12 common dream symbols (flying,
falling, water, being chased, teeth, houses, animals, death,
babies, mirrors, fire, doors) with their typical associations,
plus space to note your own personal recurring symbols.

Weekly Reviews (5 Pages)
Every 5 dreams, a review page helps you tally the week: dreams
recorded, lucid dreams, recurring themes, most vivid dream,
emotional patterns, and emerging insights.

Lucid Dreaming Progress Tracker (2 Pages)
Reality check descriptions (nose pinch, hand check, text test,
light switch), a 4-week daily reality check habit tracker, and
space to log lucid dream triggers and techniques practiced
(WBTB, MILD, WILD, DILD).

Monthly Summary (2 Pages)
Two-page monthly review: total dreams, lucid dreams, nightmares,
recurring dreams, most common emotion, top 3 memorable dreams,
recurring symbols, patterns noticed, recall improvement, and
goals for next month.
