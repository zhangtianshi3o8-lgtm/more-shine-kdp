DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------
From First Paw Print to Best Friend.

Bringing a new puppy or rescue dog home is exciting, joyful, and
sometimes overwhelming. This journal helps you stay organized and
present through every stage of training, socialization, and bonding.

With 16 weekly training session logs, a comprehensive command progress
tracker, milestone checklist, and health records, you'll have everything
you need to raise a happy, well-behaved companion — and a beautiful
keepsake of your journey together.

WHAT'S INSIDE

Dog Profile Page
Record your dog's name, breed, birthday, adoption date, microchip ID,
veterinarian, personality traits, likes and dislikes.

25-Item Milestone Checklist
Track firsts: first night, first walk, first vet visit, vaccinations,
house training, crate training, and more — with date columns.

18-Command Progress Tracker
From foundational (Sit, Down, Stay, Come) to safety (Leave It, Drop It)
to fun tricks (Roll Over, Spin, Fetch). Track date started, date mastered,
and notes for each.

16 Weekly Training Logs
Each week includes a 7-day habit tracker (training, exercise,
socialization, crate time), skills practiced checkboxes, focus and energy
ratings, what went well, and challenges to work on.

Health & Vaccination Record
Log all core vaccines (distemper/parvo series, rabies, bordetella) with
dates and next-due reminders, plus heartworm testing and prevention.

Monthly Weight & Diet Tracker
Track weight for 12 months with food brand and feeding schedule notes.
