DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------
The Ultimate Companion for Every Whiskey Tasting Journey

Whether you're a seasoned connoisseur or just beginning to explore
the world of whiskey, this beautifully designed log book helps you
capture every nuance of every pour. From peaty Islay single malts
to smooth Kentucky bourbons, your tasting journey deserves to be
documented with care.

WHAT'S INSIDE

40 Two-Page Tasting Spreads
Each session gives you space to record the whiskey name, distillery,
age, type, ABV, cask type, region, color description, and price —
plus 1-5 rating circles for peat/smoke, sweetness, oak, spice, body,
and finish length. The facing page includes a 20-note flavor checklist,
nosing notes, star rating, freeform tasting notes, and "what to
adjust next time."

Whiskey Flavor Wheel
Eight categories with 50+ tasting notes — from peat and smoke to
vanilla, caramel, dark fruit, leather, and tobacco. Find the exact
words for what you taste.

Whiskey Types Reference
Eight major styles explained: Single Malt Scotch, Blended Scotch,
Bourbon, Rye, Irish, Japanese, Tennessee, and Canadian — with
characteristics and best-for guidance.

Whiskey Regions Guide
Twelve regions profiled in detail: Scotland (Highlands, Lowlands,
Islay, Speyside, Campbeltown), Ireland, USA (Kentucky, Tennessee),
Japan, Canada, India, and emerging regions.

Tasting Terminology
Eight essential terms defined: nose, palate, finish, mouthfeel,
complexity, smoothness, peat/smoke, and oak.

Bottle Collection Tracker
Three pages to catalog your whiskey shelf — distillery, type, age,
ABV, region, and personal rating.

Distillery & Shop Log
Two pages for recording your favorite distilleries, liquor stores,
and tasting rooms. Includes a "My Go-To Shop" section.

Glassware & Accessories
One page for your tasting setup — glassware, water dropper, notebook,
and other tools.

Whiskey Regions Checklist
Tick off regions as you taste your way around the whiskey world —
from Islay to Kentucky to Yamazaki.

Year-in-Review Favorites
Summarize your whiskey year: total tastings, regions explored, top
5 whiskeys, personal discoveries, and what to explore next.

Sketch Pad & Notes
A dot-grid page for sketches and doodles, plus 10 pages of lined
notes for recipes, ideas, and reminders.
