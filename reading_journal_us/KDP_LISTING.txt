DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------
A Reader Lives a Thousand Lives.

Whether you devour a book a week or savor one a month, this journal
helps you remember every story, every character, and every idea that
moved you. From the first page to the final review, capture your entire
reading journey in one beautiful keepsake.

Each book log entry gives you space for the title, author, genre, dates,
page count, format, and source. Rate each book with stars, record your
favorite quotes with page numbers, write your review, and note your key
takeaways. With TBR wishlist pages and yearly reading statistics, you'll
always know what to read next and how far you've come.

Perfect for book lovers, book club members, students, and anyone who
believes that a good book is worth remembering.

WHAT'S INSIDE

50 Book Log Entries
Each entry includes title, author, pages, start/finish dates, genre
checkboxes (12 categories), format and source tracking, 5-star rating,
favorite quote section with page references, personal review space,
and a key takeaway box.

To Be Read (TBR) Wishlist
Multiple TBR pages with columns for title, author, genre, and priority,
so your next read is always at your fingertips.

Yearly Reading Statistics
Track books read, total pages, average rating, new authors discovered,
favorite book of the year, biggest surprise, and goals for next year.
