DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------
Train Smarter. Run Stronger.

Whether you are building base mileage, training for a marathon,
or just starting your running journey, this log helps you
record every run with purpose.

Each run uses a clean two-page spread. The left page captures
the essentials: date, distance, duration, pace, route, weather,
effort level, and heart rate. The right page gives you space to
reflect on how the run felt, what went well, what to improve,
which shoes you wore, and your workout type.

With shoe mileage tracking to help prevent injuries, race result
pages to celebrate personal records, and monthly summaries to
spot trends, this log is the perfect companion for any runner
serious about improvement.

Perfect for road runners, trail runners, track athletes, and
anyone who wants to become a better, more consistent runner.

WHAT'S INSIDE

25 Two-Page Run Log Spreads
Each run records distance, duration, pace (min/mile), route,
weather conditions, effort level (1-10), heart rate (avg/max),
and calories. The facing page provides workout type checkboxes
(Easy, Tempo, Intervals, Long, Recovery, Race), plus space for
what went well, what to improve, and goals for next time.

Season Goals Page
Set your target races and goal times for 5K, 10K, Half
Marathon, and Marathon distances at the start of each season.

Weekly Summaries (5 Pages)
Every 5 runs, a summary page tallies your week: total distance,
total time, average pace, longest and shortest runs, rest days,
and notes on the week's training.

Shoe Mileage Tracker
Track 8 pairs of shoes with start date, cumulative miles, and
retire-by mileage -- because running in worn-out shoes is the
leading cause of preventable injuries.

Race Results
Record up to 8 races with name, date, distance, finishing time,
pace, overall placement, and personal notes.

Monthly Summary
Two-page monthly review: total miles, total runs, average
distance, longest run, races completed, injuries or setbacks,
and whether monthly goals were met.
