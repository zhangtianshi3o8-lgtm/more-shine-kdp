Every seed is a link in a living chain stretching back millennia. This journal helps you become a seed saver — preserving heritage varieties, adapting crops to your garden, and building a personal seed library that grows more valuable with every season.
What's Inside
  • 40 two-page variety logs (identity + seed record)
  • Pollination guide for 15 common garden crops
  • Seed processing methods (dry and wet)
  • Germination testing protocol and viability chart
  • Storage guide for maximum seed longevity
  • 10 garden plant families with crossing rules
  • Seed library inventory (48 entries)
  • Seed swap/exchange log and acquisition wishlist
  • Year-in-review and garden layout planning pages
  • Large 6" x 9" format — easy to write in
