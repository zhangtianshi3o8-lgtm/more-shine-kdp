Preserving Heritage, One Seed at a Time

The Seed Saving & Garden Genetics Journal is the essential
companion for homesteaders, permaculturists, and heritage
gardeners who save seeds. From selecting the best mother
plants to testing germination rates and organizing a seed
library, this journal transforms scattered experience into
a reliable system that gets better every season.

WHAT'S INSIDE:

40 Variety Logs — Each variety gets a full two-page spread:
  Left page: Date, seed ID, variety name, crop/family,
  source/origin, growout record (pollination type, number
  of plants grown, isolation method, garden location),
  selection criteria checklist (flavor, yield, disease
  resistance, early maturity, size, color, drought/cold
  tolerance, storage life, pest resistance, vigor, heirloom
  purity), and mother plant description.
  Right page: Harvest details (date, processing method,
  quantity saved), processing method checklist (dry/wet/
  rinse/screen), seed quality ratings (purity, plumpness,
  cleanliness on 1-5 scale), germination test results
  (test date, number tested, percentage sprouted), storage
  location checklist (room temp/jar/fridge/freezer with
  silica gel), and observation notes.

Reference Section (6 pages):
  • Pollination guide for 15 common garden crops with
    isolation distances and minimum plant counts
  • Seed processing methods (dry threshing/winnowing and
    wet fermentation/washing) with step-by-step instructions
  • Germination testing protocol with interpretation guide
    and expected viability by crop type
  • Storage guide (4 storage methods, moisture management,
    and longevity tips)
  • 10 garden plant families with crossing rules

Seed Library Management:
  • Seed library overview inventory (48 entries, 4 pages)
  • Seed swap and exchange log (24 entries, 2 pages)
  • Acquisition wishlist and breeding/adaptation projects
  • Year-in-review favorites and lessons
  • Garden layout sketch page for isolation planning
  • 5 pages of lined notes

DETAILS:
  • 106 pages, large 6" x 9" format
  • Professional interior design with harvest gold and
    sage green accents
  • Easy-to-write-in layout with generous spacing
  • Cream paper, perfect binding
  • Suitable for beginning and experienced seed savers

Every seed saved is a link in a living chain. This journal
helps you keep it strong.
