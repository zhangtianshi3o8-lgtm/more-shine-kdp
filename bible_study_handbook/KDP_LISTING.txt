【4. 描述 Description】（KDP 商品页面展示，支持 HTML）

<i>"Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path." — Psalm 119:105</i>

<b>A complete 52-week journey through all 66 books of the Bible — with three guided study methods to keep you engaged all year long.</b>

This handbook walks you through God's redemptive story in 10 connected epochs — from Genesis to Revelation. Each week, you'll study a major Scripture passage, reflect on its theme, and discover how every part of the Bible points to Jesus.

<h2>What Makes This Handbook Different</h2>
<ul>
<li><b>3 Rotating Study Methods</b> — Most journals use just one method. This handbook cycles through SOAP, Verse Mapping, and Inductive Study every three weeks, keeping your study fresh and deepening your engagement all year long</li>
<li><b>52 Weeks of Guided Teaching</b> — Not just blank prompts. Each week includes a theme overview, key verse, reflection questions, a Christ Connection callout, and an action step</li>
<li><b>All 66 Books Covered</b> — A complete journey from Genesis to Revelation across 10 biblical epochs, with no book left behind</li>
<li><b>Anti-Bleed Design</b> — Decorative Scripture pages between weeks serve as elegant meditation breaks while preventing ink bleed-through to the next page</li>
<li><b>Built for Groups & Sundays</b> — Includes a small group discussion guide and 4 sermon notes templates for Sunday worship</li>
</ul>

<h2>What's Inside Each Week</h2>
<ul>
<li>A study page with Scripture reference, theme, key verse, and reflection questions</li>
<li>A journal page using one of three rotating methods (SOAP, Verse Mapping, or Inductive)</li>
<li>A decorative Scripture verse page for meditation and memorization</li>
<li>Gratitude prompts and prayer space on every journal page</li>
</ul>

<h2>Designed for Quality</h2>
<ul>
<li><b>8.5 x 11 inches</b> — generous writing space on every page</li>
<li><b>Wide gutter margins</b> — write comfortably near the spine</li>
<li><b>Large readable fonts</b> — minimum 10pt body text, no squinting</li>
<li><b>Translation-neutral</b> — uses verse references only, so you can study with any Bible translation (ESV, NIV, KJV, NLT, CSB, NASB, and more)</li>
<li><b>Pen guide included</b> — know which pens work best before you write</li>
<li><b>Undated format</b> — start any week of the year</li>
</ul>

<h2>Perfect For</h2>
Personal devotions, women's and men's Bible study groups, church small groups, Sunday school, and anyone who wants to go deeper into God's Word over the course of a year.

<b>10 Epochs - 66 Books - 52 Weeks - 3 Study Methods - One Story.</b>
