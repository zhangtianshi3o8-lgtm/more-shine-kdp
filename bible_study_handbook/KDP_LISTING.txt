【4. 简介 Description】（KDP商品页面展示，支持基础HTML）

<i>Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path. — Psalm 119:105</i>

<b>A complete 52-week journey through all 66 books of the Bible.</b>

This handbook guides you through God's redemptive story — from Genesis to Revelation — in 10 connected epochs. Each week, you'll study a major passage, reflect on its theme, and encounter how it points to Jesus.

<h2>What's Inside</h2>
<ul>
<li><b>52 Weeks of Teaching</b> — A full year of curated Scripture passages with theme overviews, key verses, and "Christ Connection" callouts showing how every part of the Bible points to Jesus</li>
<li><b>3 Rotating Journal Methods</b> — SOAP method, Verse Mapping, and Inductive Study keep your experience fresh all year long</li>
<li><b>Generous Writing Space</b> — Each week includes a full journaling page with scripture writing area, gratitude prompts, prayer space, and reflection questions</li>
<li><b>10 Epoch Dividers</b> — Beautiful full-page section markers tracing the biblical timeline from Creation to New Creation</li>
<li><b>Decorative Verse Pages</b> — Anti-bleed pages between weeks feature key verses in elegant typography</li>
<li><b>Small Group Guide</b> — A complete meeting format for groups using this handbook together</li>
<li><b>Sermon Notes Pages</b> — 4 bonus templates for Sunday worship</li>
</ul>

<h2>Designed for You</h2>
<ul>
<li><b>Wide margins & generous gutter</b> designed for comfortable writing</li>
<li><b>Clear pen guide</b> showing which pens work best on KDP paper</li>
<li><b>Large, readable fonts</b> (minimum 10pt) — no squinting</li>
<li><b>Translation-neutral</b> — uses verse references only, so you can study with any Bible translation you prefer (ESV, NIV, KJV, NLT, CSB, NASB, and more)</li>
</ul>

<h2>Perfect For</h2>
Personal devotions, women's Bible study groups, church small groups, Sunday school classes, and anyone who wants to go deeper into God's Word over the course of a year.

<b>10 Epochs · 66 Books · 52 Weeks · One Story.</b>
