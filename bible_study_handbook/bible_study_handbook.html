Getting Started
How to Use This Handbook

This handbook is designed to guide you deeper into God's Word through a variety of study methods. You don't need to use every method at once — choose the one that fits your current season and let the Spirit lead. Below is a brief overview of each section.

1. Bible Reading Plan
Track your progress through all 66 books of the Bible. Check off each book as you complete it.
2. SOAP Study Method
Scripture — Observation — Application — Prayer. A simple, powerful four-step method for daily study.
3. Inductive Bible Study
Observation, Interpretation, and Application for deeper, more structured study of any passage.
4. Verse Mapping
Deep-dive into a single verse: context, keywords, cross-references, and personal application.
5. Character Study
Examine the life of a biblical figure: their strengths, struggles, and what God teaches through them.
6. Topical Study
Explore what the Bible says about a specific theme across multiple books and verses.
7. Sermon Notes
Capture key points, scripture references, and action items from Sunday services and conferences.
8. Prayer Journal
Record requests, thanksgivings, and God's answers over time.
9. Gratitude
Count your blessings daily and cultivate a heart of thanksgiving.
10. Notes & Reflection
Open lined and grid pages for free journaling, sketches, and additional reflections.
Tip: There is no "right" way to use this handbook. Make it your own. Write in the margins. Underline. Doodle. This is your personal journey with God's Word.
