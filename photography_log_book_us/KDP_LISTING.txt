DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------
Capture Every Detail. Tell Every Story.

Whether you shoot landscapes, portraits, street scenes, or
wildlife, this log book helps you record every setting,
analyze your results, and grow as a photographer.

Each shoot uses a clean two-page spread. The left page
captures the technical details: date, location, camera body,
lens, focal length, aperture, shutter speed, ISO, white
balance, metering mode, and shooting mode. The right page
is for creative notes: lighting conditions, weather, time of
day, composition, what worked, what to improve, and the
story behind the shot.

With a gear inventory page, location directory, and monthly
summary, you have everything you need to turn every shoot
into a learning opportunity.

Perfect for photographers of all levels, from beginners
building their skills to seasoned artists refining their
craft. No apps, no subscriptions -- just a durable,
well-designed journal that makes you a better photographer.

WHAT'S INSIDE

25 Two-Page Shot Recording Spreads
Each shoot gives you space for date, location, camera body,
lens, focal length, aperture, shutter speed, ISO, white
balance, exposure compensation, metering mode, and shooting
mode checkboxes (Manual, Aperture Priority, Shutter Priority,
Program, Auto).

Shot Analysis Pages
Every shoot includes space for lighting conditions (Natural,
Flash, Studio, Mixed), weather, time of day, composition
notes, what worked, what to improve, the story behind the
shot, and a satisfaction rating (1-5 scale).

5 Weekly Review Pages
After every 5 shoots, a review page helps you track your
best shot of the week, biggest lesson, settings to practice,
new techniques tried, and total shots taken, plus skill
ratings for lighting, composition, and technical control.

Gear Inventory Page
Record your camera bodies, lenses, filters, tripod, flash,
remote shutter, memory cards, batteries, and accessories
in one organized place.

Location Directory
Two pages to track location names, best times to shoot,
access notes, and shots taken for up to 12 locations.

Monthly Summary
Two pages for monthly review: total shoots, total shots,
keepers, top focal length and aperture, gear used most,
favorite shots, techniques that need work, and goals for
next month.
