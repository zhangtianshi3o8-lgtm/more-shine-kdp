DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------
The Ultimate Companion for Every Pickleball Player

Whether you're a casual weekend player or a competitive
tournament regular, this beautifully designed journal helps
you track every match with precision and purpose. From your
first serve to the championship point, your pickleball journey
deserves to be documented.

WHAT'S INSIDE

52 Game Tracker Pages
Each game gives you space to record date, venue, partner,
opponents, format (singles/doubles/mixed), scoring format,
duration, weather, and a full scoring table — plus notes on
what worked, what to improve, and opponent tendencies.

Game Statistics Tracker
40-game rolling stat table tracking win/loss record, points
scored/allowed, aces, winners, unforced errors, and game
rating. See your trends across the season.

Shot Reference Guide
10 essential shots explained: serve, return, third shot drop,
dink, drive, block, lob, overhead smash, erne, and bounce-and-hit.

DUPR Rating Tracker
Track your rating progression across 20 games with differential
calculations and trend analysis.

Court & Venue Log
Log every venue you play at with surface type, court quality,
and personal notes.

Paddle & Gear Inventory
Track your paddle specs (material, weight, grip size), balls,
court shoes, and gear bag contents.
