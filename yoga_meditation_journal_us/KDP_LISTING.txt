DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------
Breathe. Move. Reflect. Grow.

Whether you are new to yoga and meditation or a seasoned
practitioner, this journal helps you track every session,
observe your inner landscape, and nurture a consistent,
meaningful practice.

Each session uses a clean two-page spread. The left page
captures the essentials: date, day, practice type, duration,
location, time of day, and your teacher or source. The right
page is for your inner experience: poses practiced, breathwork,
meditation length, how you felt before and after, energy and
mind-chatter levels, emotions that arose, insights, and
gratitude.

With intention setting, pose progress tracking, weekly and
monthly reflections, you have everything you need to turn each
practice into deeper self-awareness.

Perfect for yoga enthusiasts, meditation practitioners, and
anyone seeking more mindfulness and balance. No apps, no
subscriptions -- just a durable, beautifully designed journal
that supports your journey inward.

WHAT'S INSIDE

25 Two-Page Practice Logging Spreads
Each session gives you space for date, day, practice type
(Hatha, Vinyasa, Ashtanga, Yin, Restorative, Kundalini,
Meditation, Pranayama, Bikram), duration, location (Home,
Studio, Outdoors), time of day, and teacher or source.

Practice Reflection Pages
Every session includes space for poses practiced, breathwork
and pranayama done, meditation duration, and a full inner
check-in: how you felt before and after (1-5), energy level,
mind chatter level, emotions that arose, intuitive insights,
and gratitude from the practice.

5 Weekly Reflection Pages
After every 5 sessions, a reflection page helps you review
total practice time, the most impactful session, breakthrough
moments, challenging poses, the week's theme, and what to
focus on next.

Intention Setting Page
Define your monthly goals, sessions target, meditation goals,
and areas of focus: flexibility, strength, mindfulness, stress
relief, and spiritual growth.

Pose Progress Tracker
Two pages to track pose names, date first attempted, alignment
notes, and progress level on a 1-5 scale.

Monthly Reflection
Two pages for monthly review: total sessions, total minutes,
meditation total, new poses learned, biggest breakthrough,
areas of growth, and goals for next month.
