DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------
The Ultimate Companion for Every Baseball Season

Whether you're a die-hard fan or a casual follower, this beautifully
designed scorebook helps you capture every pitch, every hit, and every
great play of the baseball season. From opening day to the final out,
your season deserves to be documented with care.

WHAT'S INSIDE

Game Trackers with Inning-by-Inning Scoring
Each game page gives you space to record both teams, the date, the
venue, starting lineups, and full inning-by-inning scoring. Track
hits, runs, errors, and key plays as the game unfolds — your
personal record of every game you watch or attend.

Playoff Series Tracker
Follow every postseason matchup with dedicated series pages. Record
game results, runs scored, and the outcome of each series as teams
battle through October baseball on the road to the championship.

World Series Prediction Page
Make your picks before the postseason begins. Predict the matchup,
the winner, the number of games, and the MVP — then see how your
predictions hold up.

Season Predictions and Standings
Map out your season forecasts at the start and track how each
division race unfolds. Update the standings throughout the year
and see which teams exceeded expectations.

Division and Conference Reference Guides
Quick-reference pages covering every division and conference —
team names, cities, and ballpark details. A handy guide for
following the league all season long.

My Teams Page
Dedicate a page to your favorite squads. Record the teams you
follow most closely, your game-day traditions, and what makes
each team special to you.

Season Calendar and Notes Pages
Plan your viewing schedule with a season calendar, then use the
notes pages for reflections, observations, and memorable moments
from the year in baseball.
