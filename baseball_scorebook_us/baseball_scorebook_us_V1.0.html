How to Use This Scorebook
A quick guide to getting the most out of your season journal
1 Game Tracker

Use one page per game. Fill in the teams, score by inning, and record hits, runs, and errors. Note key plays and standout performances.

2 Playoff Brackets

Fill in the teams as they advance through the postseason. Track every series from Wild Card to the World Series.

3 Standings & Stats

Update team records regularly. Track batting averages, pitching stats, and your weekly picks all season long.

4 Notes & Reflections

Use the note pages to record your thoughts, memorable moments, and season highlights throughout the year.

Pro Tip

Baseball is a long season with 162 pro games. You don't have to track every one — pick the games you watch and enjoy building your personal record of the season, one game at a time!
