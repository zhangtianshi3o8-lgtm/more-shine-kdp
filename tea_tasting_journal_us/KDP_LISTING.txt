DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------
The Ultimate Companion for Every Tea Tasting Journey

Whether you're a casual tea drinker or a dedicated gongfu
practitioner, this beautifully designed journal helps you capture
every nuance of every steeping. From delicate white teas to
aged pu-erh, your tea journey deserves to be documented with care.

WHAT'S INSIDE

40 Two-Page Tasting Spreads
Each session gives you space to record the tea name, origin,
harvest date, type, processing method, brewing method, leaf grade,
water temperature, steep time, and leaf-to-water ratio — plus
a 15-note flavor checklist and freeform tasting notes on the
facing page.

Tea Flavor Wheel
8 categories from vegetal and floral to roasted and earthy,
helping you build your tea vocabulary.

12 Tea Origins Reference Guide
From China's Zhejiang province to Japan's Shizuoka, India's
Darjeeling to Sri Lanka's Ceylon — each with a characteristic
flavor profile.

Brewing Methods Guide
Gongfu, Western, Gaiwan, Grandpa Style, Cold Brew, and more —
each with description and best-for guidance.

Tasting Terminology Glossary
8 essential terms: astringency, body, huigan, qi, terroir,
finish, liquor, and infusion.
