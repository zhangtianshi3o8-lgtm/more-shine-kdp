Description:

Track Every Batch, Perfect Every Bar

The Soap Making Journal is the essential companion for cold
process and hot process soap makers. Every batch teaches
lessons — from oil ratios and lye calculations to cure
tracking and quality evaluation. This journal captures that
hard-won knowledge in one organized, professional system.

WHAT'S INSIDE:

40 Batch Logs — Each batch gets a full two-page spread:
  Left page (Recipe): Date, method, batch name, superfat %,
  batch size, oil recipe table (oil, percentage, grams,
  ounces), lye solution details (NaOH, water, concentration,
  water:lye ratio), additives and scent tracking.
  Right page (Process): Oil and lye temperatures, ambient
  temperature, time to trace, trace type checklist (none,
  thin, medium, thick, emulsion, seized), mold used, cut date,
  issues/problems, bars yielded, cure tracking table (weeks
  1/2/4/6 with zap test, pH, hardness/lather/scent), quality
  ratings (lather, scent, skin feel), and notes.

Reference Section (6 pages):
  • 14 common soap-making oils with hardness, cleansing,
    conditioning, bubbly, creamy values, and SAP values
  • Lye safety guidelines (PPE, equipment, mixing protocol)
  • Essential oil usage rates and fragrance oil behavior chart
    (stable, accelerates, rices, seizes, discolors)
  • Curing timeline with weekly water loss percentages and
    testing guide (zap test, pH strip, lather, hardness)
  • 12 common soap problems with causes and solutions
    (seize, acceleration, ricing, DOS, lye heavy, soda ash,
    partial gel, cracking, color fade, no scent, soft bars,
    glycerin rivers)

Management Section:
  • Recipe library index (36 entries across 3 pages)
  • Supply inventory (oils, lye, essential oils, colorants)
  • Gift and sales distribution log

Reflection & Notes:
  • Year-in-review with best recipes, skills, and milestones
  • 6 lined notes pages

DETAILS:
  • 104 pages, large 6" x 9" format
  • Professional interior design with lavender, herbal green,
    and honey gold accents
  • Comprehensive reference data embedded in every section
  • Cream paper, perfect binding
  • Suitable for beginner to advanced soap makers

Every batch is progress. Your best soap is the next one.
