DESCRIPTION
==========================================================

Every kick, every craving, every milestone -- capture it all.

The Pregnancy Journal is a beautifully designed 40-week guided journal that walks you through every stage of pregnancy. From the first positive test to the first cry in the delivery room, this keepsake helps you preserve every memory, track every symptom, and reflect on the extraordinary journey of bringing new life into the world.

Inside you will discover:

* 37 weekly tracking pages (weeks 4-40) with symptom checklists, mood dots, cravings, bump photo space, and guided notes
* Three trimester divider pages with guided reflections after each
* Pregnancy overview page -- due date, OB, hospital, support team
* Prenatal appointment log -- 4 pages for every visit, weight, BP, heartbeat
* Ultrasound gallery with date, measurements, and memory prompts
* Baby names brainstorm -- girl names, boy names, meaningful family connections
* Nursery planning with registry checklist
* Hospital bag checklist for mom, partner, and baby
* Birth plan with pain management, labor preferences, and C-section notes
* Baby shower planner with gifts tracker
* Birth day record -- weight, length, delivery story, first thoughts

Premium Design:
★ Elegant charcoal and gold aesthetic with heart motif
★ Generous 8" x 10" format with ample writing space
★ Symptom grids, mood trackers, and bump photo frames
★ High-quality cream paper for smooth writing

The perfect gift for newly expecting moms, baby shower presents, or a treat for yourself.
