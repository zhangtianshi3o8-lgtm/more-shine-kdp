Every dive, every depth, every adventure. This scuba diving log book gives you the space to record every detail that matters — depth, time, gas, conditions, marine life, and the gear you used. With 50 detailed two-page dive logs, a quick log, dive site tracker, safety reference, gas management guide, and gear inventory, it is everything a diver needs to build a lifetime of underwater memories.

  • 50 two-page dive logs with full profile tracking
  • Quick dive log for a bird's-eye view
  • Dive safety essentials and hand signal reference
  • Gas management guide with SAC rate calculator
  • Favorite dive sites tracker with GPS
  • Personal records and certification milestones
  • Gear inventory and service log
  • Large 6" x 9" format — easy to write in
