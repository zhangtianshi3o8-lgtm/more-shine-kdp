DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------
Every Dive, Every Depth, Every Adventure

This scuba diving log book gives you the space to record every
detail that matters — depth, bottom time, gas consumption,
water conditions, marine life encounters, and the gear you
used. Whether you dive tropical reefs, explore wrecks, or
descend into the deep blue, this journal helps you build a
permanent record of your underwater adventures.

Each two-page dive log includes space for date, time, location,
dive site, buddy, entry type (boat/shore), dive type (reef,
wall, wreck, drift, night, deep, cavern), max and average
depth, bottom time, start/end pressure with gas mix, water
temperature, visibility, current conditions, exposure
protection, weight, cylinder details, marine life spotted,
dive highlights, skills practiced, and a dive summary with
SAC rate and rating.

Perfect for certified open water divers, advanced divers,
rescue divers, dive masters, dive travelers, underwater
photographers, and anyone who cherishes their time beneath
the surface. An excellent gift for dive school graduates,
vacation divers, ocean lovers, and adventure seekers.

WHAT'S INSIDE
--------------------------------------------------------------
50 Two-Page Dive Log Spreads
Each spread captures full dive data — date, time, location,
dive site, buddy, entry type, dive type, depth profile (max
and average), bottom time, total time, gas tracking (start,
end, used), gas mix, water and air temperature, visibility,
conditions (calm, current, surge, waves), exposure protection
(suit, weight, cylinder), marine life spotted, dive highlights,
skills practiced, and a summary with SAC rate.

Quick Dive Log (4 pages)
A one-line-per-dive summary table for a bird's-eye view of
every dive — date, location, depth, time, air, temperature.

Dive Safety Essentials
Six essential safety reminders — dive planning, buddy system,
ascent rates, air monitoring, equalization, and diving within
limits.

Types of Diving Reference
Eight dive environments (reef, wall, wreck, drift, night,
cavern, deep, shore) with descriptions and depth ranges.

Essential Hand Signals
Ten standard underwater hand signals — from OK and stop to
out-of-air and low-on-air emergencies.

Gas Management Guide
Understanding air consumption with a SAC rate calculator and
typical SAC rate ranges by experience level.

Favorite Dive Sites (15 entries)
Track your best dive sites with max depth, best season, and
highlights.

Personal Records & Certification Milestones
Deepest dive, longest dive, coldest water, best visibility,
first night dive, first wreck, and certification tracking
(Open Water, Advanced, Rescue, Specialty).

Gear Inventory & Service Log
Complete equipment tracker (exposure protection, BCD, regulator,
cylinders, weights, accessories) with brand/model/size, plus
a maintenance and service record.
