DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------
The Ultimate Companion for Every Sake Tasting Journey

Whether you're a seasoned sake enthusiast or just beginning to
explore the world of nihonshu, this beautifully designed journal
helps you capture every nuance of every cup. From crisp Junmai
to aromatic Daiginjo, your sake tasting journey deserves to be
documented with care.

WHAT'S INSIDE

40 Two-Page Tasting Spreads
Each session gives you space to record the sake name, brewery
(kura), region, rice type, yeast, polishing ratio (seimaibuai),
SMV, acidity, and ABV — plus 1-5 rating circles for sweetness,
acidity, umami, body, aroma, and finish length. The facing page
includes a 24-note flavor checklist, nose aromas, star rating,
serving temperature, food pairing suggestions, and freeform
tasting notes.

Sake Flavor Wheel
Eight categories with 50+ tasting notes — from fruity and floral
(melon, apple, lychee) to rice and umami (steamed rice, lactic,
mushroom), plus cereal, sweet, nutty, earthy, spice, and other.

Sake Types Guide
Eight classifications explained: Junmai, Honjozo, Ginjo,
Daiginjo, Junmai Ginjo, Junmai Daiginjo, Nigorizake, and
Namazake — with characteristics and best-for guidance.

Sake Regions Guide
Eight major brewing regions profiled in detail: Niigata, Hyogo
(Nada), Kyoto (Fushimi), Hiroshima, Akita, Yamagata, Fukui,
and Iwate — each with its water profile, brewing tradition, and
signature style.

Tasting Terminology
Eight essential terms defined: Seimaibuai (polishing ratio),
Nihonshudo (SMV), Koji, Umami, Tanrei Karakuchi, Kura, Yamahai/
Kimoto, and Namazake — so you can speak the language of sake.

Sake Shelf Tracker
Three pages to catalog your sake collection — sake, kura, type,
polishing ratio, region, and personal rating.

Kura & Shop Log
Two pages for recording your favorite breweries, sake shops,
and izakaya. Includes a "My Go-To Shop" section.

Vessels & Accessories
One page for your tasting setup — ochoko cups, tokkuri flasks,
warming tools, and accessories.

Sake Regions Checklist
Tick off Japan's 30 prefectures as you taste your way across
the country — from Niigata to Okinawa.

Year-in-Review Favorites
Summarize your sake year: total tastings, regions explored,
top 5 sakes, personal discoveries, and what to explore next.

Sketch Pad & Notes
A dot-grid page for sketches and doodles, plus 10 pages of
lined notes for ideas, pairings, and reminders.
