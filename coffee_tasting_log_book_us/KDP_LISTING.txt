DESCRIPTION (for Amazon listing)
------------------------------------------------------------
Your perfect companion for exploring the world of coffee, one cup at a time.

Whether you are a casual coffee drinker or a dedicated home barista, this tasting log book helps you capture every detail of your coffee journey. From single-origin beans to your favorite espresso blend, record what makes each cup memorable.

<b>What's Inside:</b>

  - 50 detailed tasting entry pages with guided prompts
  - Cupping scoresheet with the SCA-inspired 100-point scale
  - Brewing method tracker (pour-over, French press, AeroPress, espresso, and more)
  - Coffee origin reference map with major growing regions
  - Flavor wheel reference guide to sharpen your palate
  - Roast level and grind size quick-reference chart
  - Bean inventory and purchase log
  - Personal rating and favorites summary pages

<b>Designed for Coffee Lovers:</b>

Each tasting page guides you through the essential evaluation steps — aroma, flavor, acidity, body, sweetness, and aftertaste. The clean layout gives you plenty of writing space without crowding, and the compact 6" x 9" format goes wherever your coffee adventures take you.

<b>Perfect For:</b>
  - Home baristas honing their craft
  - Coffee enthusiasts tracking their favorite roasts
  - Cupping sessions and tasting groups
  - Gifts for the coffee lover in your life
