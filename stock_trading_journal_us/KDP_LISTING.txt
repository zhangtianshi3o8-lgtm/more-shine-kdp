DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------
Trade With Discipline. Review With Honesty.

Whether you trade stocks, ETFs, or options, this journal helps
you track every position, analyze your decisions, and build a
data-driven edge in the market.

Each trade uses a clean two-page spread. The left page captures
the setup: ticker, direction, entry and exit prices, position
size, stop loss, target, risk amount, P&L, and R multiple. The
right page is for analysis: your thesis, what went right, what
went wrong, mistakes, lessons learned, and a confidence and
emotional control check.

With a trading plan page, watchlist tracker, and monthly
summary, you have everything you need to turn raw trade data
into actionable insight.

Perfect for active traders, swing traders, and anyone serious
about improving their performance. No apps, no subscriptions --
just a durable, well-designed journal that makes you a better
trader.

WHAT'S INSIDE

25 Two-Page Trade Recording Spreads
Each trade gives you space for ticker, direction (long/short),
entry and exit prices, shares, stop loss, target, risk amount,
P&L, R multiple, and percent return. Plus setup type, market
context, and outcome checkboxes.

Trade Analysis Pages
Every trade includes space for your entry thesis, what went
right, what went wrong, rule violations, key lessons, a chart
sketch area, and confidence and emotional control scoring
(1-5 scale).

5 Weekly Review Pages
After every 5 trades, a review page helps you calculate win
rate, net P&L, average win, average loss, profit factor, and
identify recurring patterns and areas to improve.

Trading Plan Page
Define your account size, max risk per trade, daily loss
limit, setup criteria, risk management rules, and exit rules
before you place a single trade.

Watchlist Tracker
Two pages to track tickers, sectors, setups, target entries,
and stop levels for upcoming trades.

Monthly Summary
Two pages for monthly performance review: net P&L, win rate,
profit factor, expectancy, and goal setting.
