DESCRIPTION
------------------------------------------------------------
The perfect companion for every camping adventure.

Whether you're pitching a tent at a national park, parking
the RV at a favorite campground, or backpacking into the
wilderness, the Camping Journal helps you capture every
moment — from the first tent stake to the last ember of
the campfire.

This thoughtfully designed journal includes:

• 35 two-page camp log spreads with space for trip
  details, weather, camp type, campground info, and
  memorable highlights
• Campsite sketch and map area for each trip
• Wildlife spotted and camp conditions tracker
• Favorite campsites quick-reference page
• Camping summary for year-in-review totals
• Leave No Trace seven principles reference
• Camping essentials checklist (10 categories)
• Comprehensive packing checklist (11 categories)
• Emergency contact and medical info page

Built for durability in a pack-friendly 5" x 8" format,
this journal is ready for every starry night, every
campfire memory, and every outdoor adventure.

Published by More Shine Press.
