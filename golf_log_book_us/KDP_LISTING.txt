DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------
The Ultimate Companion for Every Round of Golf

Whether you're a weekend golfer or a dedicated player chasing a
lower handicap, this beautifully designed log book helps you
capture every shot, every score, and every great moment on the
course. From the first drive to the final putt, your golf
journey deserves to be documented with care.

WHAT'S INSIDE

40 Rounds with Full 18-Hole Scorecards
Each round gives you a complete scorecard for all 18 holes —
par, stroke play, and stableford scoring, plus space for
fairways hit, greens in regulation, putts, penalties, sand
saves, and driving distance. Record course name, date, weather,
tee color, playing partners, and overall notes for the round.

Stat Tracker for Fairways, Greens, and Putts
Track your performance round by round with dedicated stat
tracking — fairways hit, greens in regulation, total putts,
scrambling, sand saves, and penalty strokes. Spot trends and
improve your game.

Season Goals and Handicap Tracking
Set goals at the start of the season and track your progress.
Monitor your handicap index, record best rounds, and celebrate
milestones throughout the year.

Club Distance Reference
A personal reference chart for every club in your bag — driver,
fairway woods, hybrids, irons, wedges, and putter. Know your
exact yardages and make confident club selections.

My Courses Log
Catalog the courses you've played — name, location, par,
yardage, slope rating, and your best score. Build a personal
record of every course in your golfing journey.

Golf Terminology and Game Formats Guide
Essential golf terms defined and explained, plus a guide to
popular game formats — stroke play, match play, stableford,
scramble, best ball, and more.

Season Calendar and Stat Summary
Plan your season with a month-by-month calendar, then review
your year with a comprehensive stat summary — total rounds,
average score, best round, fairways hit percentage, putting
average, and handicap progression.
