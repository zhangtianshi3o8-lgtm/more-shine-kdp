DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------
Not All Those Who Wander Are Lost.

From weekend city breaks to month-long backpacking adventures, this
travel journal helps you plan every trip, record every moment, and
preserve your memories for a lifetime.

Each trip gets a full three-page spread: page one for essentials
(destination, dates, companions, transportation, accommodation),
page two for a daily itinerary planner, and page three for highlights,
food discoveries, ratings, and a detailed expense tracker with budget
vs actual comparisons.

With a 24-destination bucket list and a countries-visited tracker,
you'll always have your next adventure in sight.

Perfect for solo travelers, couples, families, backpackers, and
anyone who believes the journey is the destination.

WHAT'S INSIDE

15 Three-Page Trip Spreads
Each trip captures destination, country, dates, duration, trip type,
transportation checkboxes (8 options), travel companions, weather,
accommodation details, pre-trip notes, and reservations.

Daily Itinerary Planner
Seven-day grid for morning, afternoon, and evening plans, plus a
must-see places and bookings section.

Memories & Expense Tracker
Best moments, food and drink discoveries, 5-star overall rating, and
a 7-category expense table (flights, lodging, food, local transport,
activities, shopping, other) with budget vs actual columns and total.

Travel Bucket List
24 dream destinations from Northern Lights to Machu Picchu to the
Maldives, plus space for your own additions.

Countries & Places Visited Tracker
Log every country visited with year and highlights.
