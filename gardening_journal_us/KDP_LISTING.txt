DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------
The Perfect Companion for Every Gardening Season

Whether you tend a sprawling backyard plot, a few raised beds,
or a collection of containers on the patio, this journal helps
you capture your garden's story from first seed to final harvest.
Track every planting, every bloom, and every harvest through
all four seasons.

WHAT'S INSIDE

35 Two-Page Garden Entry Spreads
Each entry gives you space for date, season, weather conditions,
soil moisture, tasks completed (planted, watered, weeded, pruned,
fertilized, harvested, composted, mulched), a plant log with five
rows for varieties and quantities, time spent, and helpers — plus
a garden sketch area, growth observations, pest and disease log,
harvest tracker, challenges, and enjoyment rating on the facing
page.

Companion Planting Guide
Eight tried-and-true plant pairings that grow better together,
from tomato and basil to marigold and everything in between.

Garden Essentials Checklist
Ten must-have tools every gardener needs, with a brief tip for
each.

Season Summary
Tally your total gardening days, hours, plants started, plants
harvested, total harvest weight, most successful crop, biggest
challenge, and top 5 crops of the season. Set goals for next year.

Planting Calendar
Quick reference for when to plant favorite crops by season —
spring, summer, and fall.
