DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------
Protect Your Investment. Every Mile, Every Service.

This vehicle maintenance log book is your car or truck's complete
service history in one place. Whether you do your own oil changes
or take it to a trusted mechanic, this journal keeps every repair,
part number, and cost organized for years to come.

Each service entry uses a clean two-page spread: the left page
captures date, mileage, service type, work performed, and parts
used. The right page breaks down costs (parts, labor, tax), tire
condition by position, and notes for next time.

With fuel and mileage tracking pages, you can calculate MPG over
time and spot trends in your vehicle's performance. Annual summary
tables help you see the big picture each year.

Perfect for car owners, truck owners, DIY mechanics, and anyone
who wants to protect their vehicle's resale value.

No apps, no subscriptions. Just a durable, well-designed log book
that lives in your glovebox.

WHAT'S INSIDE

30 Two-Page Service Log Entries
Each entry gives you space for date, mileage, service type, shop
or DIY, a five-row work-performed table with part numbers and
costs, and fluid level checks.

Cost & Tire Tracking
The right page of each entry includes parts/labor/total breakdown,
a five-position tire condition table (tread depth, pressure,
brand, replace flag), and space for observations and reminders.

Fuel & Mileage Log
Six pages of fuel tracking with 14 fill-ups per page -- date,
mileage, gallons, price per gallon, total cost, and calculated MPG.

Vehicle Information Page
Year, make, model, VIN, license plate, engine specs, insurance,
preferred mechanic, tire size, and oil type.

Annual Summary Tables
Every 10 services, a summary page totals your costs by category,
tracks annual mileage, and notes plans for next year.
