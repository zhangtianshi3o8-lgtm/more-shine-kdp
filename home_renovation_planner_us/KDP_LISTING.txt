DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------
Plan Smart. Build With Confidence.

Whether you are remodeling a kitchen, updating a bathroom, or tackling
a full home renovation, this planner keeps every project organized
from concept to completion.

Each project uses a clean two-page spread: the left page captures
project scope, room details, measurements, and a materials shopping
list. The right page tracks your budget (budgeted vs. actual by
category), contractor details, progress log, and lessons learned.

With a home overview page and a contractor directory, you have
everything you need to manage renovations of any size.

Perfect for homeowners, DIY enthusiasts, and anyone planning home
improvement projects. No apps, no subscriptions -- just a durable,
well-designed planner that keeps your projects on track.

WHAT'S INSIDE

25 Two-Page Project Planning Spreads
Each project gives you space for scope of work, project type
(kitchen, bathroom, flooring, electrical, etc.), start/target dates,
measurements, and a 5-row materials table with quantities and costs.

Budget Tracker
Every project includes a budgeted vs. actual cost breakdown across
6 categories: materials, labor, permits, tools, disposal, and misc.
A total row shows your variance at a glance.

Home Overview Page
Property address, year built, square footage, priority renovation
list, overall budget, and key contractor contacts.

Contractor Directory
Two pages to store trades, company names, phone numbers, and ratings
for every contractor you work with.
