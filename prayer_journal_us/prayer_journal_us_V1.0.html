How to Use This Journal

This journal is designed to guide you through a year of intentional prayer. Each week offers a fresh Scripture passage and space to pray using the ACTS model.

1
Find a quiet moment. Set aside a regular time each week — morning, evening, or whenever you can be still before God. Even five minutes is enough to begin.
2
Read the weekly Scripture. Let the verse settle in your heart before you start writing. Read it slowly, perhaps more than once.
3
Pray through the ACTS sections. Write your prayers using the four guided categories on the left page. There are no right answers — be honest with God.
4
Reflect on the right page. Use the space for personal prayers, people you are praying for, and what God is teaching you.
5
Record answered prayers. Use the special section in the back of this journal to track how God has responded. Looking back builds faith.
6
Don't worry about perfect weeks. If you miss a week, simply pick up where you left off. Prayer is a relationship, not a checklist.

The ACTS Prayer Model

A simple framework used by Christians for centuries
A Adoration
Praise God for who He is. Reflect on His character — His love, holiness, power, mercy, and faithfulness. Tell Him what you admire about Him.
C Confession
Be honest about where you have fallen short. Ask for forgiveness, knowing that God is faithful and just to forgive. Release anything weighing on your conscience.
T Thanksgiving
Express gratitude for God's blessings — both big and small. Thank Him for His provision, protection, people in your life, and above all, for His grace.
S Supplication
Bring your requests to God. Pray for others first — family, friends, leaders, those in need. Then bring your own needs, trusting that He hears and cares.
ACTS is a guide, not a rule. You may spend more time in one area on some weeks and less in others. The goal is simply to draw near to God with an open heart.

A Year of Prayer

Prayer is one of the greatest privileges of the Christian life — the invitation to speak with the Creator of the universe and to listen for His voice. Yet for many, prayer can feel difficult, distracted, or dry.

This journal exists to help you build a consistent, meaningful prayer life over the course of fifty-two weeks. Each week, a Scripture passage will anchor your prayers. Guided sections will help you move beyond routine requests into deeper conversation with God.

As you write, you will create a record of your spiritual journey — the struggles, the breakthroughs, and the quiet faithfulness of God. When you look back, you will see a story of grace that you may have missed in the moment.
