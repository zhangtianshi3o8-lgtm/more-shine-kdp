DESCRIPTION (for Amazon listing)
------------------------------------------------------------
Deepen your prayer life one week at a time.

This guided prayer journal walks you through fifty-two weeks of intentional communion with God. Each week features a carefully selected Scripture passage to anchor your heart, along with generous writing space organized around the time-tested ACTS prayer model — Adoration, Confession, Thanksgiving, and Supplication.

Whether you are new to prayer or have walked with God for decades, this journal provides a simple, meaningful structure that helps you:

  •  Spend focused time in God's Word each week
  •  Pray with intention using the ACTS framework
  •  Track prayer requests and see how God answers
  •  Cultivate a grateful heart through guided reflection
  •  Look back on a full year of spiritual growth

The journal is undated — start any week of any year. Its elegant, timeless design works beautifully for both men and women, making it a thoughtful gift for anyone seeking to draw closer to God.

FEATURES:
  •  52 undated weekly spreads (two pages per week)
  •  52 Scripture passages from the World English Bible
  •  Guided ACTS prayer sections on every weekly spread
  •  Dedicated "Answered Prayers" section (4 pages)
  •  Gratitude & Thanksgiving reflection pages (4 pages)
  •  Free-form notes & reflection pages (6 pages)
  •  Generous writing space that respects your handwriting
  •  Premium 6" x 9" format — portable yet spacious
  •  Elegant navy and gold design

A perfect companion for your daily walk with God — or a meaningful gift for a friend, family member, pastor, or small group member.
