DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------
Cast On With Confidence. Finish Every Project.

Whether you knit sweaters, scarves, socks, hats, blankets, or
gifts, this planner helps you track every project from first
stitch to final bind-off. Stay organized, record what works,
and never lose track of a pattern or gauge swatch again.

Each project uses a clean two-page spread. The left page
captures the essentials: project name, pattern source,
designer, yarn details, needle and hook sizes, gauge swatch,
start and target dates, and project type. The right page is
for your notes: stitch patterns, adjustments, problems and
solutions, notions, care instructions, and difficulty and
satisfaction scoring.

With needle and hook inventory, yarn stash tracking, a pattern
wishlist, and a handknit gift log, you have everything you
need to keep your knitting life organized.

Perfect for knitters of all levels, from beginners to
experienced makers. No apps, no subscriptions -- just a
durable, well-designed planner that helps you finish what you
start.

WHAT'S INSIDE

25 Two-Page Project Spreads
Each project gives you space for name, pattern source,
designer, recipient, size, start and end dates, project type
checkboxes (sweater, scarf, hat, socks, blanket, shawl,
mittens, toy), yarn details (brand, weight, color, dye lot,
fiber, skeins), and a gauge swatch box with needle and hook
sizes.

Project Notes Pages
Every project includes space for stitch patterns used, cast-on
stitches, total rows, total time, adjustments and
modifications, problems encountered and solutions, buttons and
notions, care instructions, and difficulty and satisfaction
scoring (1-5 scale).

5 Progress Review Pages
After every 5 projects, a review page helps you track projects
finished, projects in progress, total knitting hours, favorite
project, new techniques learned, biggest challenges, and goals
for the next period. Includes a 7-day habit tracker.

Knitting Goals Page
Define your yearly goals, projects to make, new techniques to
learn, gifts planned, yarn budget, and your challenge project.

Needle & Hook Inventory
Two pages to track every needle and hook by US size, metric
size, type, length, material, and quantity.

Yarn Stash Tracker
Two pages to log your yarn by brand, weight, color, fiber
content, skeins, and total yardage.

Pattern Wishlist
One page to keep a running list of patterns to try, with
designer, type, difficulty, and whether you have tried it.

Handknit Gift Log
One page to track handknit gifts by project, recipient,
occasion, date given, and their reaction.

Notes Pages
Three blank lined pages for project ideas, pattern sketches,
and free notes.
